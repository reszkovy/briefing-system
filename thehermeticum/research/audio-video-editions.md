# Audio & Video Editions of Hermetic Texts and Scholarship

Research dossier for The Hermeticum. All URLs accessed 2026-08-14. YouTube view counts are approximate snapshots as of that date and will drift; treat them as popularity proxies only.

Editorial framing: this dossier separates **FACT** (verifiable, sourced) from **EDITORIAL** (our assessment). A recurring legal point governs everything below: the *texts* are ancient, but a recording reads a specific *translation*. Mead (1906) and Everard (1650) are public domain; the Kybalion (1908) is public domain as a text. **Copenhaver (1992, Cambridge UP) and Salaman et al. (1999) are in copyright — recordings of those translations are unauthorized and must not be linked.** Freke & Gandy's *The Hermetica* (1997) is both in copyright and a loose paraphrase, failing on two counts.

### 1.2 Asclepius (Perfect Sermon)

**FACT:** No standalone LibriVox or major-channel full reading found. A dedicated audio reading of Mead's Asclepius exists: "The Perfect Sermon, or the Asclepius (Ancient Hermetic Text — Audio Reading)", https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=THGJCc5FOZg (translation: Mead, from *Thrice-Greatest Hermes*, per description). The Asclepius is also included inside the Copenhaver-based Master Key Society recording (see 1.1 — not linkable) and inside complete Mead *Thrice-Greatest Hermes* recordings (e.g. Altrusian Grace's Vol. 2 material; their Vol. 3 reading with text: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6PHU91noIcM).

**EDITORIAL:** Weakest audio coverage of the four source texts. Best legal pointer: the Mead-based reading above, or the Audible *Thrice-Greatest Hermes* (Schmitz). Salaman's widely-liked *Asclepius: The Perfect Discourse* (2007) has no authorized audio; scans circulating on archive.org are unauthorized — don't link.

### 1.3 Kybalion (1908)

**FACT:**

Catalog page: https://librivox.org/the-kybalion-by-the-three-initiates/ (page returned HTTP 403 to our fetcher but is indexed and mirrored); Internet Archive mirror verified: https://archive.org/details/kybalion_1412_librivox.
- Master Key Society, "The Kybalion (1908) by Three Initiates": ~5M views — one of the most-viewed esoteric audiobooks on YouTube. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aL43l2SFVWQ
- Altrusian Grace Media, "THE KYBALION — A Study Guide For Hermetic Philosophy — Full esoteric audiobook w/ Text + Images": ~2.2M views. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Etz2Kgtev5w (also on Bandcamp and as an Audible edition narrated by Matthew Schmitz, https://www.audible.com/pd/The-Kybalion-Audiobook/B0BXQ5JKFJ)
- A trade-published audiobook also exists (Macmillan Audio, narr. Peter Ganim, 2019: https://www.amazon.com/The-Kybalion-Hermetic-Philosophy/dp/B082VM46N9).

**EDITORIAL:** Legally unproblematic (public domain text) but editorially loaded: the Kybalion is a 1908 New Thought work, not a hermetic source text, and its 5M+ view counts vs. the Corpus Hermeticum's hundreds of thousands quantify the site's core problem — the modern pseudo-Hermetica out-circulates the ancient corpus roughly 10:1 in audio. Recommend the LibriVox recording *with* our standard framing ("listen to it as a 1908 American document"), never as an entry point to Hermeticism.

### 1.4 Emerald Tablet

**FACT:** The text is a single page; no long-form "audiobook" is meaningful. Findings (YouTube search, 2026-08-14):
- Altrusian Grace Media, "The Emerald Tablet Of Hermes — REMASTERED — a brief history and 3 translations": ~14K views. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uK7hLq8QAc4
- ESOTERICA (Dr. Justin Sledge), "What is the Emerald Tablet of Hermes Trismegistus — Origins of Alchemy and Hermetic Philosophy": ~707K views — includes the text and its actual (Arabic) textual history. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5qa4woIz_Ag
- Long tail of low-quality "Emerald Tablet" videos, incl. algorithm-bait ("I Read the Emerald Tablet Page They Deleted…", 19K views in 2 days, 2026-08) and frequent conflation with Doreal's *Emerald Tablets of Thoth the Atlantean* (1930s channeled text) in compilation audiobooks (e.g. audiobooks.com "Hermeticism Classic Collection" bundling Doreal with the Corpus Hermeticum: https://www.audiobooks.com/audiobook/hermeticism-classic-collection-corpus-hermeticum-the-kybalion-the-emerald-tablets-of-thoth-the-atlantean-the-life-and-teachings-of-hermes-trismegistus/731520).

**EDITORIAL:** For the Emerald Tablet, the best "listen" is not a reading but Sledge's episode — it delivers the text plus the philology in 30 minutes. Flag the Doreal conflation explicitly wherever we mention Emerald Tablet audio: bundles that shelve Doreal's Atlantean fantasy next to the Corpus Hermeticum are the single most common audio-format misinformation vector we found.

## 4. Editorial verdict — "Listen instead" section

**RECOMMEND (in this order):**
1. **ESOTERICA "Hermetic Philosophy and Hermeticism" playlist (61 videos)** — start with "Corpus Hermeticum — Introduction" (19 min). Best free, rigorous on-ramp; same editorial values as ours.
2. **SHWEP episodes 102–109** — the Poimandres, Asclepius/Korê Kosmou, Litwa and Bull interviews. The serious deep-dive companion to our Ancient Texts section.
3. **Altrusian Grace Media, "The Divine Pymander" (Everard 1650, 3h27m)** — legal full reading of the first English Corpus Hermeticum; frame as "hear the 1650 translation Newton's contemporaries read."
4. **Mead-based Corpus Hermeticum readings** (e.g. Man of Letters w/ on-screen text, 3h10m) — legal; warn about Mead's Theosophical-era diction.
5. **"The Perfect Sermon, or the Asclepius" (Mead) audio reading** — the only dedicated legal Asclepius audio we found.
6. **LibriVox Kybalion (Andrea Fiore, ~4h)** — with framing: a 1908 New Thought document, not ancient Hermetica.
7. **Litwa, *Hermetica II* (Audible)** — the one officially-audiobooked scholarly volume; fragments and testimonia.
8. **Embassy of the Free Mind / Hanegraaff lectures + "Infinite Fire" webinars** — free university-grade lectures.

**ADVISE AGAINST (with stated reasons, not silent omission):**
- **Any recording of Copenhaver's or Salaman's translations** (incl. the 387K-view "Hermetica: Greek Corpus Hermeticum and Latin Asclepius" upload) — unauthorized readings of in-copyright translations; we don't link piracy.
- **Freke & Gandy "Lost Wisdom of the Pharaohs" audiobooks (2.4M views)** — in copyright, and a rearranged paraphrase marketed as the ancient text.
- **Compilation audiobooks bundling Doreal's *Emerald Tablets of Thoth the Atlantean*** with the Corpus Hermeticum — 1930s channeled material shelved as an ancient source; the most common audio misinformation vector found.
- **Algorithmic "forbidden Emerald Tablet" content** — engagement-bait with no textual basis.
- Generic no-name "Corpus Hermeticum" Audible/YT editions that do not disclose the translation — undisclosed translation = unverifiable rights and unverifiable text.

## CONTENT HOOKS

1. **"The most-listened Hermetica isn't the Hermetica."** The Kybalion (5M+ views) and Freke & Gandy's paraphrase (2.4M) out-circulate every genuine Corpus Hermeticum reading roughly 10:1 — a data-backed piece on how audio platforms rewrote the hermetic canon.
2. **"Why there is no legal audiobook of the best translation."** Copenhaver (1992) has no authorized audio, so the field's standard text circulates as piracy while Mead's 1906 Theosophical prose dominates legal audio — a copyright story that shapes what people believe Hermeticism sounds like.
3. **"Listen to 1650."** The Everard *Divine Pymander* reading as a time machine: the exact English text through which the 17th century met Hermes — pairs with our Renaissance/early-modern section.
4. **"Thoth vs. the Atlanteans."** How audiobook bundles quietly shelf Doreal's 1930s channeled *Emerald Tablets* next to the ancient corpus — a short explainer that would rank for the most-confused query in the niche.
5. **"A university lecture hall with no door fee."** Curated map of the Amsterdam cluster (HHP chair, Embassy of the Free Mind, Infinite Fire webinars) + SHWEP + Esoterica as a complete free audio-visual curriculum in academic Hermeticism.
