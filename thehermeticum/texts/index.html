The Texts

TL;DR

Nine bodies of text, three languages, seventeen centuries. Start with the Corpus Hermeticum — or with the one “hermetic” bestseller that isn’t ancient at all.

  • Corpus HermeticumThe seventeen treatises at the heart of the tradition (c. 100–300 CE).
  • — PoimandresThe vision that opens the Corpus, guided.
  • The Emerald Tablet“As above, so below” — the real, Arabic origin.
  • AsclepiusThe perfect discourse — and the lament for Egypt.
  • The Kybalion (1908)What it gets right, and what it invents.
  • Definitions of HermesThe oldest philosophical Hermetic text — in Armenian.
  • Stobaean FragmentsIncluding the Korē Kosmou.
  • Nag Hammadi HermeticaThe 1945 desert finds — proof of ritual practice.
  • PicatrixThe Arabic handbook of astral magic.
  • Musaeum HermeticumThe Baroque alchemical anthology.

How this site is made. The Hermeticum is a compilation: its pages are assembled with AI research tools from published scholarship — the editions, monographs and papers named in the sources of every entry. Nothing here is original historical research, and nothing is presented as revelation. Where scholars disagree we say so; where a claim is contested we mark it. Errors are ours and worth reporting — see Method for how we source, and how we correct.
