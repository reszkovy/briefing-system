Fair questions

Before you ask

Is this a religion or something I’m “joining”?

No. This is the history of ideas — read the way a good university seminar would read it, minus the tuition. No initiations, no memberships, no robes.

Do I need to believe in magic to enjoy this?

Not at all. You need the same thing you’d bring to Greek mythology or the history of alchemy: curiosity. What people believed, why they believed it, and what it changed — that’s the story.

Why would a modern person care?

Because these texts helped shape the Renaissance, early modern science, Jung’s psychology and half of pop culture’s image of “secret knowledge”. Knowing the real story beats repeating the fake one.

Is this like the TikTok manifestation stuff?

It’s the antidote. Same fascination, opposite method: primary sources, named scholars, honest “we don’t know” where the evidence ends.

How this site is made. The Hermeticum is a compilation: its pages are assembled with AI research tools from published scholarship — the editions, monographs and papers named in the sources of every entry. Nothing here is original historical research, and nothing is presented as revelation. Where scholars disagree we say so; where a claim is contested we mark it. Errors are ours and worth reporting — see Method for how we source, and how we correct.
