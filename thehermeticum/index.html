Why hermeticism

Hermeticism begins with a demanding idea: transformation requires seeing the relation between an inner state and an outer system. The Hermeticum translates that tradition into contemporary practice — without pretending that ancient texts predicted AI.

  1. IOne thingAll things arise from one, by adaptation.
  2. IIAs above, so belowThe levels mirror each other — so the work is possible.
  3. IIISeparate the subtlePart the fine from the gross — gently, with great skill.
  4. IVAscend & descendRise from earth to heaven, return — and hold the force of both.

Fair questions

Before you subscribe

Is this another productivity system?

No. Several ideas overlap — systems, energy, intention — but the aim is different. Productivity optimises output; this optimises presence and judgement, and will happily cost you output to protect them.

Do I have to believe in Hermeticism?

Not at all. It is the source language of the method, read as history of ideas. You can hold every metaphysical claim at arm’s length and still use the practice.

Do I need to use AI?

No. The practice works on paper. If you do use AI, it gives you a position of your own before the model has one for you.

Where is my data stored?

In your browser, and nowhere else. Nothing is sent to us or to any model unless you export it yourself. The e-mail you give for the letter is kept by our mail provider and nothing else.

Why is The Hermeticum anonymous?

To take the ego out of the middle. The text matters more than a face, the sources more than a persona — and every claim here is checkable without knowing who wrote it.

What is free, and what will be paid?

Knowledge stays open: the sources, the archive, the beginning of the path, the glossary, the letter and selected practices. Structure, tools and guided practice may be paid later — never access to the ideas themselves.

How this site is made. The Hermeticum is a compilation: its pages are assembled with AI research tools from published scholarship — the editions, monographs and papers named in the sources of every entry. Nothing here is original historical research, and nothing is presented as revelation. Where scholars disagree we say so; where a claim is contested we mark it. Errors are ours and worth reporting — see Method for how we source, and how we correct.
