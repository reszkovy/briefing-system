Why hermeticism

Hermeticism is two thousand years of texts circling one idea: what happens inside you and how your surroundings work are connected — so changing one changes the other. We take the way of thinking and leave the prophecy. Nobody here pretends that ancient texts predicted AI. The four lines below are the oldest record of this method; the rest of the site is what it looks like in use today.

  1. IOne thingAll things arise from one, by adaptation.
  2. IIAs above, so belowThe levels mirror each other — so the work is possible.
  3. IIISeparate the subtlePart the fine from the gross — gently, with great skill.
  4. IVAscend & descendRise from earth to heaven, return — and hold the force of both.
