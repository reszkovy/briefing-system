How this site is made. The Hermeticum is a compilation: its pages are assembled with AI research tools from published scholarship — the editions, monographs and papers named in the sources of every entry. Nothing here is original historical research, and nothing is presented as revelation. Where scholars disagree we say so; where a claim is contested we mark it. Errors are ours and worth reporting — see Method for how we source, and how we correct.
