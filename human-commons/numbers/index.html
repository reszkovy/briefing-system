Phase 2 · Spring 2028 · cost €18–37k by region · revenue €27–36k

The test residency — two weeks, 15–20 people

Venue is the variable; everything else is constant. Exclusive-use, ~18 beds, off-season (April–May or October), 14 nights. Market quotes: comparable masserie/villas for 16 guests run €8.5–13k per week in season, with off-season discounts of 20–40%; mid-range agriturismi host 20-person retreat weeks at €8–15k all-in.

Pricing sanity check: €1,800 for two weeks is deliberately below market — corporate offsites benchmark at $3,500–6,500 per person for a few days; week-long European founder retreats commonly price €1,500–3,000. The first cohort pays for proof, not margin.

Entry gate — demand pays first

Seats are sold before any deposit is paid. Open applications, collect payments; only at 12+ confirmed participants is the venue booked. If it doesn't sell, the thesis got a cheap, honest answer — and the loss is one scouting trip. Abruzzo venue economics make the test nearly risk-free; Puglia buys stronger photography and an easier yes from participants.

Phase 4 · 2029+ · €0.5–1.5M · external capital

Ownership — bought with data, not hope

Only after two seasons of numbers: occupancy, retention, what people say at the last dinner. Then the purchase is of a priced business, not a dream — and that is when first believers come in. Separately and privately: the founder's own house (≤€100k) is bought regardless, as the personal hedge — worst case, the project dies and there is still a home in a village worth loving.

V

Three rules that hold it together

01

Demand pays before we do

Every phase has an entry gate based on sales, not enthusiasm. No deposit leaves the account until participants' deposits have arrived first.

02

Culture first, walls last

Stone is the cheapest part of this project; community is the most expensive. We buy buildings only when the culture already fills them.

03

Maximum loss is written down in advance

Through the end of 2028, total exposure stays within ~€30–35k — an amount that can go to zero without touching the life it is meant to improve.
