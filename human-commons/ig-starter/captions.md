## Posty 01–12

**01 · plate-hill (obraz — wioska we mgle)**
> Plate I — The hill, at first light.
>
> This account documents an experiment: a real village, in Italy, designed for the age when
> intelligence is abundant — and human life becomes the scarce thing worth building for.
> It does not exist yet. That is the point. Follow along as we build it in public, from the
> first sketch to the first long dinner.

**02 · quote-machines (cytat, ciemny)**
> The machines will do the work. We intend to do the living.
>
> The founding sentence of the commons. Everything we post here unfolds from it.

**03 · plate-table (obraz — długi stół)**
> Plate II — The table, set for twenty.
>
> The only piece of infrastructure that matters. The rest of this project is architecture
> around one long dinner that nobody wants to end.

**04 · quote-luxury (cytat, jasny)**
> The rarest luxury of the coming age will not be intelligence.
>
> Intelligence is becoming infrastructure — like electricity, like running water. What stays
> scarce: attention, presence, craft, an evening nobody wants to end.

**05 · plate-morning (obraz — biurko o poranku)**
> Plate III — The quiet hours.
>
> The daily rhythm of the village: mornings belong to the future — deep work with AI,
> research, building, writing. Then the screens close.

**06 · quote-mornings (cytat, ciemny)**
> The mornings belong to the future. The rest of the day belongs to being human.
>
> Nobody checks. Everybody knows.

**07 · campus-map (obraz — mapa)**
> Fig. 1 — First campus, working plan.
>
> Piazza at the heart, because conversation needs a stage. A long table beside it, because
> trust is built at dinner. Forno, amphitheatre, baths, a quiet house, an olive press, a
> belvedere for the last conversation of the day. The dotted circle is the whole world you
> need on foot. And look at the gate: the cars stay outside.

**08 · quote-culture (cytat, jasny)**
> AI does the work. Humans create culture.
>
> Not a rejection of technology. A demotion — beneath life, not in front of it.

**09 · plate-soil (obraz — ręce w ziemi)**
> Plate IV — Afternoon, the soil.
>
> Some questions are best asked with your hands in the soil. Gardens are not decoration here
> and walking is not exercise — both are how thinking happens.

**10 · quote-community (cytat, ciemny)**
> No networking. Just community.
>
> Residents are not customers. They are members — chosen not for what they own, but for what
> they bring to the table.

**11 · plate-door (obraz — uchylone drzwi)**
> Plate VI — The door is not locked. It never was.
>
> We are gathering the first twenty people: founders, researchers, designers, craftspeople.
> If something in you has been waiting for a page like this — the manifesto is in the bio.
> A person reads every letter.

**12 · quote-infrastructure (cytat, jasny)**
> Technology becomes infrastructure. Human life becomes the point.
>
> We exist to take that sentence literally — with stone, soil, and a very long table.
> Field notes from Italy begin in October.
