## 04 · quote-luxury (karta jasna)

The rarest luxury of the coming age will not be intelligence.

Intelligence is becoming infrastructure — like electricity, like running water.
Nobody will boast about having it. What stays scarce is everything it cannot make:
attention, presence, craft, an evening nobody wants to end.

We are building a place that takes this literally. Italy, first.

#postaisociety #humancommons #newvillage

## 05 · plate-morning (obraz — biurko o poranku)

Plate III — The quiet hours.

The daily rhythm of the village: mornings belong to the future — deep work with AI,
research, building, writing. One long stretch of concentration while the light is still cold.

Then the screens close, and the rest of the day begins.

#postaisociety #humancommons #deepwork

## 06 · quote-mornings (karta ciemna)

The mornings belong to the future. The rest of the day belongs to being human.

Nobody checks. Everybody knows.

This is the only rule of the day here — and the reason the village needs a workshop,
a garden and a very long table more than it needs another meeting room.

#postaisociety #humancommons #newvillage
