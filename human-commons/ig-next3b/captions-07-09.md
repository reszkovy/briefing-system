# @postaisociety — posty 07–09 (pełny kadr 4:5, bez letterboxu)

## 07 · campus-map (obraz — plan wioski)

Fig. 1 — First campus, working plan.

A piazza at the heart, because conversation needs a stage. A long table beside it,
because trust is built at dinner. Forno, amphitheatre, baths, a quiet house, an olive
press, a belvedere for the last conversation of the day.

The dotted circle is the whole world you need on foot. And look at the gate:
the cars stay outside.

## 08 · quote-culture (karta jasna)

AI does the work. Humans create culture.

Not a rejection of technology — a demotion. Beneath life, not in front of it.

Every tool we use has one job here: to give the day back to the people living it.

## 09 · plate-soil (obraz — ręce w ziemi)

Plate IV — Afternoon, the soil.

Some questions are best asked with your hands in the ground. Gardens are not decoration
here and walking is not exercise — both are how thinking happens.

The machines can answer. They cannot notice.
