01 / 12 The Post-AI Society
Concept briefing · MMXXVI

The machines will do the work.
We intend to do the living.

A network of physical places for the AI era — beginning with one Italian hill village. This is a concept, presented before the first stone, to the people we want beside us.

02 / 12The Post-AI Society The shift

AI is about to make productivity abundant. Abundant things stop being valuable.

IntelligenceAttention
ContentConversation
ProductivityPresence
Connection, nominalBelonging, real

Every prior platform shift created new scarcity and the fortunes went to whoever owned it: attention (media), distribution (internet), trust (brands). The next scarcity is embodied human experience.

03 / 12The Post-AI Society The thesis

The future luxury is not AI.
It is a life lived mostly off-screen.

Technology becomes infrastructure. Human life becomes the point. We invest in the places where that sentence is physically true: piazzas, gardens, workshops, one long table.

Not a coworking brand. Not a retreat business. A prototype of a way of living — with the durability of real estate and the margins of a community brand.

04 / 12The Post-AI Society
What we are building

A village where AI does the mornings
and humans own the rest of the day.

Deep work until lunch. Craft, gardens and sport in the afternoon. One long dinner every evening — lectures, music, conversation. Twenty residents at first, chosen for what they contribute, not what they own.

05 / 12The Post-AI Society Why now

Two curves are crossing, and they cross in Italy.

Curve one — demand

The AI displacement of knowledge work

The people with the most to gain from this thesis — founders, researchers, creators — are the first to feel their calendars emptying. They are actively searching for what comes next.

Curve two — supply

Empty, beautiful, subsidized Italy

Thousands of depopulating borghi; municipalities running programs to attract new residents; entire restored hamlets renting for less than a city office floor. The land is asking for this project.

The window

Cheap before it is obvious

Both curves are visible today and priced by almost no one. When "human-scale living" becomes a category, the honey-colored stone will not be cheap anymore.

06 / 12The Post-AI Society The path

We are not launching a village. We are compounding one.

Phase I · 2026Test residency

2 weeks, ~15–20 paying participants, rented agriturismo. Proof of rhythm, proof of demand, first film and photography.

Phase IISeasonal campus

Long-term lease of a hamlet section. Recurring residencies, 20 beds, cowork, workshop, kitchen.

Phase IIIThe village

Permanent community, families, school of craft. First acquisitions once the model proves itself.

Phase IVThe network

One culture, one membership, many tables — beyond Italy, in time.

Asset-light until the model is proven: phases I–II run on leases and partnerships, not acquisitions. The playbook — rhythm, admissions, governance, kitchen — is the asset that scales.

07 / 12The Post-AI Society Business model — concept

Three streams, one long table.

01 · Residencies

Paid stays, 2–12 weeks

The engine of the early years. Revenue from day one of Phase I; every residency also produces alumni, content and demand for the next one.

02 · Membership

The network card

Recurring membership across locations — closer to a guild than a gym. Admission by contribution. Retention driven by belonging, the one thing subscription businesses can't fake.

03 · Media & playbook

The journal, the method

Essays, film, and the operating playbook itself — licensed to partner properties that want a Post-AI Society season without us owning their walls.

Concept-stage: unit economics will be validated by the Phase I test residency, priced to break even at ~15 participants.
