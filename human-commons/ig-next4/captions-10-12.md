## 10 · house-after (obraz — wizualizacja dziedzińca) · ETYKIETA AI: TAK

**Field note 01 — the building.**

Four hundred years old. Empty for thirty. Somewhere between Abruzzo and Puglia there are
hundreds of them: too big for one family, too beautiful to fall down, too expensive for the
village that is left.

This is what one of them could look like by the second spring. Not restored into a hotel —
uncovered. Lime instead of cement. Chestnut instead of laminate. A long table where the cars
used to turn around.

To be clear: this is a visualisation, not a photograph. We would rather show you the standard
we are holding than promise it in words.

In October we go and walk through the real ones.

#postaisociety #humancommons #newvillage #alberghediffusi

## 11 · first-dinner (obraz — chleb, ręce) · ETYKIETA AI: TAK

**Field note 02 — dinner number one.**

We already know how the first evening goes.

Someone arrives late because the train from Rome is always the train from Rome. Bread has
been in since four; nobody planned that, one person simply could not sit still. Twenty people
who chose each other, at one table, with no agenda and no name badges.

By eleven the conversation has split into three. By one, two people are still on the steps
arguing about something that will matter to them for years.

That evening is the entire product. Everything else — the building, the money, the plan —
exists only to make it possible.

Spring 2027. Two weeks. Twenty seats.

#postaisociety #humancommons #longtable

## 12 · the-same-sentence (karta) · ETYKIETA AI: NIE

**Field note 03 — the same sentence, twice.**

There are around six thousand half-empty villages in Italy. Every one of them was emptied by
the same sentence: the work went away. The press closed, the terraces stopped paying, and
everyone under thirty took a train north to where the factories were.

Seventy years later the sentence comes back, and this time it is being said about the
factories, the offices, the studios, the desks. The work is going away again.

The difference is the direction of travel. Last time, people had to follow the work. This
time they do not.

A village emptied by automation, refilled by automation. We do not think that is irony.
We think it is the whole opportunity.

#postaisociety #humancommons #spopolamento
