Milestone achieved

After more than a year of work, you can say – we have it! We are able to identify microorganisms responsible for the development of tuberculosis and other mycobacteriosis in humans using the LC-MS / MS method.

During the research, we determined characteristic biomarkers and their profiles, which constitute the basis of an innovative diagnostic method prepared for implementation. The key advantages of our solution are:

– identification of microorganisms from the group Mycobacterium tuberculosis complex (MTBc) and others (MOTT)!

The figures present the same results in several ways, showing the data related to the identification of microorganisms using the LC-MS / MS method – pie chart, PCA analysis, heatmap and bar charts for selected groups of microorganisms.
