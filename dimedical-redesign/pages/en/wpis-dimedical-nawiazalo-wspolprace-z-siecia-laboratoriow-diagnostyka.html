DiMedical has established a partnership with the Diagnostyka laboratory network

We are pleased to announce that we have begun cooperation with Diagnostyka, the largest provider of laboratory diagnostic services in Poland. As a result, the M-Typer diagnostic test, which uses tandem mass spectrometry (FIA-MS/MS) technology, will become available to a significantly wider group of patients and clinicians.

This partnership is an important step in bringing our technology from the research laboratory into routine diagnostic practice.
