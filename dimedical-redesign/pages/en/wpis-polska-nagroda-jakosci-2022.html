Polish Quality Award 2022

The Polish Quality Award is an economic award granted to companies that demonstrate high management standards in their working environment and offer clients products or services of the highest quality.

In November 2022 DiMedical Clinical Medicine Centre received the Polish Quality Award granted by PAP.
