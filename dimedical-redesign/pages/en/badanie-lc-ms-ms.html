LC-MS/MS — M-Typer

M-Typer: Advanced Mycobacterial Diagnostics

Waiting time Up to 24 h

When should the test be performed?

LC-MS/MS – M-Typer is an innovative diagnostic method that uses high-performance liquid chromatography coupled with tandem mass spectrometry (LC-MS/MS) to identify Mycobacterium tuberculosis complex and atypical mycobacteria. Indications for performing the test include:

  • Suspected pulmonary or extrapulmonary tuberculosis
  • Diagnosis of atypical mycobacterial infections in patients with symptoms suggestive of mycobacteriosis
  • Rapid species-level identification of isolated mycobacteria
  • Diagnostics in cases where conventional microbiological methods yield inconclusive results
  • Analysis of mycolic acid structures in scientific and epidemiological studies

Test description

The LC-MS/MS – M-Typer method enables detailed analysis of mycolic acids, which are unique components of the mycobacterial cell wall. This technology allows for:

  • Sensitive and specific identification of Mycobacterium tuberculosis complex and atypical mycobacteria
  • Quantitative and qualitative analysis of mycolic acids, providing precise information about their length and structure
  • Rapid diagnostics without the need for lengthy bacterial cultures
  • Classification of mycobacteria to the species level based on their lipid profile

Diagnostic significance

LC-MS/MS – M-Typer represents a breakthrough in the diagnosis of mycobacterial diseases, allowing for highly accurate and sensitive identification of mycobacteria. This method enables the rapid differentiation between tuberculosis and other mycobacterioses, which is essential for selecting appropriate treatment and limiting disease transmission.

Test results

Results are available electronically or for in-person collection. The Center for Clinical Medicine offers comprehensive mycobacterial diagnostics using state-of-the-art analytical technologies, ensuring the highest quality of laboratory testing.
