Business Boxing Night

Yesterday, another edition of the Biznes Boxing Night Gala organized by Biznes Boxing Polska took place in the Teatr club in Lodz. Biznes Boxing Polska is the largest organization of White Collar Boxing in Poland. During each event, money is collected for a charity through auctions held during the gala and individual collections of players. So far, more than PLN 2,058,000 has been collected for charity. The DiMedical Clinical Medicine Center sponsored this event.
