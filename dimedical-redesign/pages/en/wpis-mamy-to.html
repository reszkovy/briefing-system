We did it!

Based on the latest advances in analytical chemistry (mass spectrometry), the DiMedical laboratory has developed an innovative method that detects tuberculosis bacilli directly from clinical material, without the need for lengthy culture.

The method relies on sensitive and specific detection of mycobacterial cell wall components, which form a kind of molecular fingerprint unique to each microorganism. This makes it possible not only to detect tuberculosis bacilli at high sensitivity (50 cells/mL), but also to determine their strain and drug susceptibility. Diagnostics will be shortened from 4 weeks to 3 hours.

This revolutionary technique will help shorten the diagnostic process not only in Poland but above all worldwide. We are one step closer to solving a problem humanity has faced for tens of thousands of years. We are building the diagnostics of the future.
