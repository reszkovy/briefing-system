Diagnostics pioneer

Centrum Medycyny Klinycznej DiMedical Sp. z o.o. is the work of one man – MD, PhD. Karol Majewski, laboratory diagnostician, microbiologist specializing in infectious diseases, who implements his visionary plans for the development of the company in an entrepreneurial way. He made a world breakthrough in tuberculosis diagnostics. As a specialist in clinical microbiology, he wants to spread access to his discovery.

Dr. Majewski’s enterprise is developing in two ways. The first part of the activity is an aesthetic medicine clinic, where innovative methods of performing procedures such as laser therapy or lipotransfer are used. The second part is activity based on clinical medicine. – The idea came about a few years ago, when I was running the Provincial Tuberculosis Diagnostic Unit at the Provincial Hospital for Lung Diseases – says Karol Majewski. – Every year we have at least 10 million new cases of tuberculosis in the world, of which 2-3 million people die. In Poland, it is approx. 7 thousand. new cases every year. Currently, the profile of people suffering from tuberculosis has also changed. It is no longer associated with low social and financial status, but with reduced immunity of people who live fast and under high stress. That is why the number of cases among CEOs, doctors and lawyers is increasing – he adds. (…)
