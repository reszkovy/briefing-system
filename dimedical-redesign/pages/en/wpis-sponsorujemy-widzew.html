We sponsor the Lodz Widzew!

DiMedical joined the group of Widzew Lodz partners at the beginning of the current season, and in October 2021 it was the Strategic Sponsor of Widzew Lodz in the 67th Derby of Lodz. Now the cooperation has been significantly expanded – DiMedical will be the Strategic Sponsor of Widzew Lodz throughout the spring round of the current season.
