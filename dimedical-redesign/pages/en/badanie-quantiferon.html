Quantiferon-TB Gold Plus

Modern Diagnosis of Latent Tuberculosis

Waiting time 24 h

When should the test be performed?

Quantiferon-TB Gold Plus is an advanced, sensitive, and specific indirect test used to detect latent Mycobacterium tuberculosis infection. Indications for performing the test include:

  • Contact with a person infected with tuberculosis bacilli
  • Suspected latent TB infection in individuals at risk (immunocompromised patients, cancer patients, people with HIV)
  • Prior to initiating immunosuppressive therapy
  • Diagnostics in individuals with a positive tuberculin skin test (TST)
  • Screening of healthcare workers and other professionals at risk of exposure

Test description

The Quantiferon-TB Gold Plus test measures the release of interferon-gamma by T lymphocytes stimulated with Mycobacterium tuberculosis-specific antigens. It uses the ESAT-6 and CFP-10 antigens, which are absent in BCG vaccine strains and most non-tuberculous mycobacteria (except M. szulgai, M. marinum, M. kansasii).

  • Unlike the tuberculin skin test (TST), the Quantiferon-TB Gold Plus test is not subject to subjective interpretation and does not produce false positives due to prior BCG vaccination
  • It offers higher sensitivity and specificity
  • The analysis is performed on peripheral blood samples, eliminating the need for multiple patient visits

Diagnostic significance

Quantiferon-TB Gold Plus allows for effective identification of individuals infected with tuberculosis bacilli while minimizing false positives common in skin tests. It is especially useful in diagnosing latent TB in high-risk groups and in screening programs.

Test results

Results are available electronically or for in-person collection. The Center for Clinical Medicine offers high-quality diagnostics based on modern immunological testing, ensuring reliable interpretation of results.
