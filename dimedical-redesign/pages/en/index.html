DiMedical Clinical Medicine Centre

DiMedical Clinical Medicine Center is an innovative biomedical company operating in the field of clinical medicine, laboratory diagnostics, research and development (R&D), using modern medical technologies. The company was established in 2016 in Lodz, and its main goal is to develop and implement unique diagnostic methods in the field of infectious and cancerous diseases. Find out more

Recognition

Awards and certificates

Since 2018 our work has been regularly recognised by industry bodies, quality programmes and international control systems.

2018

Polish Smart Development Award

In 2018 DiMedical Clinical Medicine Centre won the Polish Smart Development Award in the category "Medicine and pharmacy of the future".

2019

Polish Innovation Award 2019

DiMedical was nominated for the "Polish Innovation Award 2019" in the medicine category, for a project implementing an innovative method of diagnosing tuberculosis and other mycobacterioses.

2021

Fundament Award

We are delighted that our company was once again recognised by the Council of the Polish Entrepreneurship Congress. The Fundament 2021 Award was granted for pro-social activities contributing to the development of SARS-CoV-2 detection.

2020

Fundament Award 2020

Nasza firma została laureatem nagrody Fundament 2020. Istotną rolę w uzyskanym wyróżnieniu odegrał projekt dotyczący szybkiej diagnostyki gruźlicy. Ową nagrodę traktujemy jako swoiste potwierdzenie, że obrany przez nas kierunek pracy jest dobry i zauważalny w branży.

2020–2022

EQA SARS-CoV-2 certificates

DiMedical met the requirements of the International External Quality Assessment (EQA) Programme for SARS-CoV-2. The verified diagnostic method was RT-PCR.

2021

QCMD 2021

DiMedical took part in the QCMD 2021 SARS-CoV-2 EQA Programme, confirming the reliability of diagnostic procedures for SARS-CoV-2 genome detection.

2022

Polish Quality Award 2022

In November 2022 DiMedical received the Polish Quality Award granted by PAP.

2021

MADE IN POLAND programme

The MADE IN POLAND Congress showcases the potential of Polish manufacturers who shape and develop the Polish economy across industries.

2019–2022

POLMICRO / SSE

Our laboratory met the requirements of the POLMICRO/SSE programme, confirming the high quality of microbiological diagnostics performed at the centre.
