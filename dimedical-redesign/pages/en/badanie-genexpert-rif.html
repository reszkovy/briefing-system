GeneXpert MTB/RIF

Rapid Diagnosis of Tuberculosis and Rifampicin Resistance

Waiting time Up to 24 h

When should the test be performed?

GeneXpert MTB/RIF is an advanced, automated diagnostic method based on PCR technology. It is used for the rapid detection of Mycobacterium tuberculosis and identification of resistance to rifampicin, a key drug in tuberculosis therapy. Indications for performing the test include:

  • Suspected pulmonary or extrapulmonary tuberculosis
  • Diagnosis of patients with symptoms such as chronic cough, hemoptysis, fever, and weight loss
  • Suspected multidrug-resistant tuberculosis (MDR-TB)
  • Urgent need to initiate anti-tuberculosis treatment
  • Tuberculosis diagnosis in immunocompromised patients (e.g., HIV/AIDS, immunosuppressive therapies)

Test description

GeneXpert MTB/RIF is one of the fastest methods for identifying tuberculosis and its resistance to rifampicin, providing results in as little as 2 hours. The test involves amplification of Mycobacterium tuberculosis genetic material in a closed system, minimizing the risk of contamination and increasing result reliability.

  • The analysis is performed on sputum and other clinical specimens (e.g., body fluids, biopsies)
  • The fully automated system eliminates the need for multi-step laboratory processing
  • The test detects the presence of tuberculosis bacilli and mutations associated with rifampicin resistance
  • Rapid results allow for immediate initiation of an appropriate treatment regimen

Diagnostic significance

GeneXpert MTB/RIF is recommended by the World Health Organization (WHO) as a primary diagnostic tool in cases of suspected tuberculosis and MDR-TB. With its high sensitivity and specificity, the test significantly shortens diagnostic time, which is crucial for controlling disease spread and initiating effective treatment.
