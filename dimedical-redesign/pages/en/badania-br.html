About the project

DiMedical runs, among others R&D project no. POIR.01.01.01-00-0798 / 16 under the title „Research and development works in the field of developing an innovative method of diagnosing tuberculosis and other mycobacteriosis”, which aims to implement a high-throughput, sensitive, cheap and fast diagnostic method, based on the analysis of mycolic acid profiles, which are part of the structure of the cell wall of bacteria of the Mycobacterium genus. The main goals of this project are to develop methods of biotyping and assessing drug resistance of microorganisms of the genus Mycobacterium (M.tb complex and MOTT), therefore they focus on the diagnosis of the etiological factor and not the markers of the response of an infected patient.

Scope

Main project goals

High-throughput method

Deployment of a sensitive, inexpensive and fast diagnostic method based on mycolic acid profile analysis.

Biotyping

Development of biotyping methods for microorganisms of the Mycobacterium genus (M. tb complex and MOTT).

Drug resistance assessment

Development of methods for assessing mycobacterial drug resistance — key to selecting effective therapy.

R&D project no. POIR.01.01.01-00-0798/16. The project is co-financed by the European Union.
