M-Typer

Rapid mycobacterial diagnostics using FIA-MS/MS

An innovative diagnostic technology developed by DiMedical, using tandem mass spectrometry to identify bacteria of the genus Mycobacterium — including mycobacteria of the Mycobacterium tuberculosis complex and non-tuberculous mycobacteria (NTM).

The technology is based on the analysis of mycolic acids — specific lipids of the mycobacterial cell wall that serve as a characteristic biochemical marker for this group of microorganisms. The solution was created for diagnostic laboratories, reference centres, scientific institutions and implementation partners.

Technology

What is M-Typer technology?

M-Typer uses mycolic acids as markers for the presence and differentiation of bacteria of the genus Mycobacterium.

Mycolic acids are long-chain fatty acids that constitute a key structural component of the mycobacterial cell wall. Their profile differs between species and groups of mycobacteria, which is why it can be used for the identification and differentiation of mycobacteria.

01

Isolation of mycolic acids

Extraction of the characteristic cell-wall lipids of mycobacteria from the tested material.

02

FIA-MS/MS analysis

Flow injection analysis coupled with tandem mass spectrometry.

03

Profile interpretation

Analysis of the result using dedicated M-Typer Software.

The technology can support the detection of infections caused by mycobacteria of the genus Mycobacterium, including the Mycobacterium tuberculosis complex and non-tuberculous mycobacteria.

Process

How does M-Typer work?

The process consists of five key stages — from sample preparation to result interpretation.

1

Sample preparation

Biological or culture-derived material is prepared for mycolic acid isolation. In the case of sputum, the procedure includes prior preparation and liquefaction of the sample.

2

Isolation of mycolic acids

Using the M-Typer Reagent Kit: washing, alkaline hydrolysis, neutralisation, liquid–liquid extraction and preparation of the extract for analysis.

3

FIA-MS/MS analysis

The extract is analysed by flow injection analysis with tandem mass spectrometry, monitoring selected MRM transitions characteristic of mycolic acids.

4

Mycolic acid profiling

The system generates an analytical profile of the mycolic acids present in the tested sample.

5

Result interpretation

The profile is interpreted using dedicated M-Typer Software, which supports the analysis of the obtained result.

Infrastructure

Instrument compatibility

M-Typer has been developed for laboratories equipped with high-sensitivity triple quadrupole tandem mass spectrometers operating in ESI ionisation mode and enabling FIA-MS/MS analysis with MRM transition monitoring.

SCIEX QTRAP® 6500Triple quadrupole tandem mass spectrometer
Shimadzu LCMS-8050Triple quadrupole tandem mass spectrometer
Equivalent systemsOther LC-MS/MS systems meeting the analytical requirements of the method

If a laboratory has a triple quadrupole LC-MS/MS system, M-Typer may provide an opportunity to extend its diagnostic portfolio with mycolic acid profile analysis for bacteria of the genus Mycobacterium.

Product

M-Typer Reagent Kit

An in vitro diagnostic medical device intended for the isolation of mycolic acids from biological or culture-derived material for subsequent FIA-MS/MS analysis. The kit supports the sample preparation stage and enables the generation of an extract containing mycolic acids.

Complete reagent kit

The M-Typer Reagent Kit contains all reagents required for the isolation of mycolic acids from biological or culture-derived material. It enables 100 isolations and is an integral part of the M-Typer workflow before FIA-MS/MS analysis.

Used by qualified laboratory personnel in accordance with the current Instructions for Use.
